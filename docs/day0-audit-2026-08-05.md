## 1. What was applied, and what stayed gated

`CLAUDE.md` §5 as amended 2026-08-05 splits the regime, and the discriminator is **what the
falsifying evidence is** — not how settled the conclusion is:

- **APPLIED DIRECTLY** — the falsifying evidence is a **fact ID or a dated first-hand observation
  of a value that was read** (a DOM field, a `/bots` footer, a hidden-input payload, a picker's
  option list). §5 condition 3 is satisfiable and **no decision changes**.
- **GATED** — the falsifying evidence is **a ruling recorded in another project document.** That
  fails §5 condition 3 (*"Never another project document: two documents vouching for each other is
  a citation loop"*) however settled the ruling is. This is what keeps D-3's `itmlive` gate,
  Decision 6's Template V2, and the §2D scoping count **out** of the applied set.
- **Also gated:** anything that **adds or reorders a Day-0 step**, adds a delete instruction, or
  changes a disposition (all failure branches are dispositions). `build-plan.md` in any part.
  **Ambiguous → gated.**

**8 edits applied directly** (F-11, F-19, F-20, F-21, F-22, F-30, F-31, plus the F-24 checklist
half). **28 findings gated**, with ready-to-paste amendment text below. Every applied edit is
listed in §D with its anchor, evidence and verification hash for Andy's commit review — **Andy's
veto is at commit, not before.**

> ⚠️ **One caution on the gated set.** The D-1 precedent (`reactivation-runbook.md:118`) shows this
> project treats even a *substring replacement* carrying a ruling as requiring explicit release.
> Nothing below that carries a ruling was applied on that precedent.
