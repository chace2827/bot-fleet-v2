## T-17 — RESOLVED. The question was moot, and the answer is better than the question.

**What T-17 asked** (`post-u1-package-2026-08-07.md` §4.1): U-1 recorded "an Automation Log link"
on Trades-list rows but never tested **whether that link's target names the closing object for an
Exit-Option close**. §4.1 predicted it "plausibly does not for Exit Options (a bot-level bundle)"
and noted that settling it "would reopen this ruling on much better terms."

**Method.** `oa-platform-reference.md` §0.2 forbids inference from absence, so this was a positive
DOM query, not an eyeball. Target: `Friday 14 DTE Broken Wing IB (B-70)`, position SPX Iron
Butterfly exp Sep 11, whose close is a known **Exit-Option** close.

**Observation, verbatim from the Trades block:**
```
Close 1 contract - Sep 3, 2026 12:16PM
Profit Target: $105.00 profit at $39.00
38.95 → 39.00
filled at $38.95
Open 1 contract - Aug 28, 2026 10:30AM
Automation Log
40.05 → 39.85
filled at $40.05
```
DOM: `document.querySelectorAll('a.link.tlink').length === 1` for the whole drawer, and that single
anchor sits on the **OPEN** row.

**Finding: an Exit-Option close row carries NO Automation Log link at all.** The count of 1 proves
this is a real absence, not a selector miss. In the link's place the row carries the **exit rule
itself, with its threshold**: `Profit Target: $105.00 profit at $39.00`.

**Consequence — this reopens G-1' exactly as §4.1 anticipated.** The degraded schema was
`(exit_ts, fill_price)` with `row_type` **hand-assigned**. It is not hand-assigned. The close row
is self-labelling, and the three labels are machine-distinguishable:

| Close-row label | Means | Link? |
|---|---|---|
| `Profit Target: $X profit at $Y` · `Stop Loss: -N% of debit at mid price` | an **Exit Option** fired, threshold included | **no** |
| `Automation Log` | a **monitor/automation** fired — link names it | **yes** |
| `Auto close ITM position before expiration` / `Market` | **OA platform safety net**, not a bot rule | **no** |

⚠️ **Whether that reopens the exit_rows.csv DECLINE is a RULING and is GATED.** Recorded here as an
observation only. `R-2026-08-07-G-1'` is untouched.

## F5 extension — the DIR exit design is ASYMMETRIC, and only one side is unmodeled

Yesterday's M-36 F5 read one CallVIXdrop position and found a platform auto-close. Two more have
now been read. **F5 as written stands; this extends it.**

`DIR-SPX-CallVIXdrop` — 3 of 8 closed positions reachable from `/positions/closed` (the account
list caps at 90 rows; the other 5 predate that window).

| # | Opened | Closed | Structure | P/L | Close-row label | Quote pair? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 3 | 11:00AM | **3:50PM** | Long Call Spread 7710/7725 | **+$425** | `Auto close ITM position before expiration` / `Market` | **NO** |
| Sep 2 | 11:00AM | 1:18PM | Long Call Spread 7670/7685 | −$310 | `Stop Loss: -51.3% of debit at mid price` | `2.95 → 2.90`, filled $3.00 |
| Aug 27 | 11:00AM | 2:28PM | Long Call Spread 7720/7735 | −$365 | `Stop Loss: -54.5% of debit at mid price` | `3.05 → 3.00`, filled $3.10 |

**The finding: losers have an exit, winners do not.**
- **Downside is governed.** The `-50%` STOP LOSS % Exit Option fires, at a **mid-price limit**, with
  a readable quote pair. It works.
- **Upside is ungoverned.** No profit target is configured, so a winning 0-DTE debit spread rides to
  expiry and is disposed of by OA's ITM auto-close as a **market order** at 3:50PM.

**Two consequences worth Andy's attention:**
1. **Slippage is measurable on the loss side and NOT on the win side.** Stop-loss closes record a
   bid/ask pair; the auto-close records only `Market`. So the one exit path with unmodeled execution
   cost is also the one path OA gives no data for. It cannot be quantified from this surface —
   Tradier fills or a broker record would be needed.
2. **The stop overshoots its setting.** Configured `-50%`; fired at **-51.3%** and **-54.5%**. On
   $610–$675 of debit that is roughly $8–$30 per contract beyond the threshold. Small at 1 contract,
   linear in size.

**Corroborating read — the bot Log (`/bots/bot/<id>/log`).** ~50 `Entry Scanner` runs at 11:00AM
daily back to Jun 26. **Zero exit entries of any kind.** The bot's only automation is the entry
trigger; every exit is an Exit Option or the platform. Consistent with the table above.

`DIR-SPX-PutVIX22-SL75` shares the identical shape (Entry Scanner 11:00am, no bot inputs,
"Generated from a backtest") and has **never filled** (F6), so it inherits the same asymmetry
untested.
