### ⚠️ INCIDENT, DISCLOSED AND CORRECTED — a wrong tag was written and then removed
Typing `PR-12` into `60min-ORB-10W-Paper-v1` and pressing Enter committed **`pr 11`**, not
`pr 12`: the tag widget's autocomplete had highlighted the existing `pr 11` suggestion, and Enter
takes the *highlighted suggestion*, not the typed literal. Detected immediately by the post-edit
model re-read. Corrected in the same session: `pr 12` added, then `pr 11` removed via the chip's
own `onRemoveTag` control; the bot now reads exactly `mirror watch,pr 12`, and `Trendy-Paper-v1`
still holds the only `pr 11`. **Method note for the runbook: with this widget, verify the stored
tag after every single Enter — the keystroke is not the value.**
