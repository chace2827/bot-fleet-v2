falsifies in writing:

1. The guard says *"The 08-31 capture read `GF-QQQ-IC-Ride-Delta` as already 1 per day / 1 at a
   time."* It did not. `06-ride-delta-scanner-diff-2026-08-31.md` §5 reads, verbatim:

   conflated.
2. A GF condor is **two OA positions**: ScannerA opens the put spread and ScannerB the call
   spread, as separate actions on the same day (`data/trades.csv` 2026-08-20 T00345, both sides).
   Read today first-hand: every GF arm is at 2/2.
3. The same 08-31 forensics state the consequence of a budget of two being consumed by one side:
   *"the opposite side is blocked for the rest of the day … it DESTROYS THE CONDOR, leaving a naked
   one-sided position."* At **1** per day, one side is blocked **every** day, by construction.

So OA's counter at 2/day already **is** "1 condor per day" — the unit CLAUDE.md §4 makes the unit
of account, and the unit `PR-14…PR-20 MAX LOSS` uses ("1 condor/day"). Setting the raw counter to 1
would not implement Andy's instruction; it would halve every condor on seven bots.
Sizing and OA bot behaviour are **always gated** (`CLAUDE.md` §5, derived-ruling authority), and
"when it is ambiguous, it is gated". The edit is therefore **withheld and escalated**, not made.
